Nepal's first community-driven R&D laboratory dedicated to EV batteries and sustainable materials research.
The Battery Innovation Lab serves as a hub for research, testing, and knowledge exchange. We provide state-of-the-art facilities for battery diagnostics, second-life system development, and materials analysis.
Our lab welcomes researchers, students, entrepreneurs, and industry professionals who want to contribute to Nepal's circular economy transition.

Arrival of Lab Equipments on Battery Innovation Lab

The Battery Innovation Lab received its first set of equipment, with the purchase process facilited by Impact Hub. The equipment included design and fabrication kits, testing equipments, BMS modules and all the necessary PPE equipment.
